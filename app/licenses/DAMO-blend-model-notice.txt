AURA runs the reviewed network locally with torch.load(weights_only=True),
strict state dictionaries, evaluation mode and FP32 inference.
The learned 512px blend map is interpolated and applied to current pre-wrinkle
original-resolution FP32 RGB with eye/lash/brow/hair/dark-mark protection.
No extra whitening, old eye luminance-lift formula, cheek-colour transfer,
or uint8 rounding is applied by this stage. All wrinkle strengths default to 0.
This is a bounded regional adaptation, not the entire ABPN pyramid pipeline,
not a dedicated newly trained wrinkle classifier, and not PixCake private code.
